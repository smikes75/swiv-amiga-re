THIS IS NOT AN EMULATOR

An emulator boots the original program inside a simulated Amiga and never needs to understand what is on the disk. This page is the opposite: it understands the disk itself and runs none of its code.

  • reads the custom bootblock chain and the game’s own catalogue — the disk has no filesystem
  • decompresses all three proprietary packers (two boot-time, one streaming) re-implemented from disassembly
  • decodes the graphics formats natively: 9 full screens, ~100 sprite sets, 934 frames, palettes, music metadata
  • everything happens in your browser — nothing is uploaded, nothing copyrighted ships with this page

Game data © 1991 Storm / The Sales Curve. Bring your own disk image (.adf) of S.W.I.V. — it stays on your machine.
