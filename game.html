S.W.I.V. playable reconstruction

IN-PROGRESS RECONSTRUCTION. Maps, graphics, palettes, scroll tempo and enemy positions come straight from your original disk. All 1497 map objects in 73 kinds are transcribed, TOWN through FINAL, and so is the whole sound engine — every effect is synthesised the way the original does it, from the routines in AMPROG.OBJ. Newest: the post-game statistics screen, the title/hi-score music switch and a fix to the boss-death synth. Follow the measured progress in the repo.
